## Day 49 – Exam strategy

*LFCS · Exam Practice*

### Goal

Plan time, flags, and verification habits.

### Concept

- Read whole task list first
- Easy points first
- After each change: **verify** (`systemctl status`, `getent`, `ip a`, `df`)
- Don’t fight SELinux/firewall without checking logs
- Leave 15 minutes to re-check services

### Why This Matters

Strategy saves failed-but-almost tickets.
