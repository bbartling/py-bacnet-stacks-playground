# Day 112 — Final Review — router field demonstration and contribution

[Previous: Day 111](day111.md) · [Course index](INDEX.md) · [Lab guide](LAB_GUIDE.md)

**Week 16 review · 2–4 hours, split across sessions as needed.** Prerequisites: Days 1–111, or equivalent skills. Reuse your earlier work rather than start every tool again.

## Project brief

Explain one BACnet request end to end and deliver one bounded contribution to the unfinished DIY BACnet Router or its correct upstream owner. Combine the course’s Rust tools, packet reasoning and evidence habits. Completion means a credible, reproducible contribution and an honest capability report—not finishing every product milestone.

Environment: Read-only repository study first; software lab and optional isolated Pi/RS-485 bench. Follow the destination repository contract before edits. Apply the [review rubric](LAB_GUIDE.md#review-rubric). This page intentionally contains no worked solution, implementation sequence, or companion implementation. Pick your own decomposition. You may consult language/API references and your earlier work.

## Acceptance criteria

- Trace a read-only request through client, B/IP wrapper, NPDU forwarding, destination transport and application response, with captured/source evidence.
- Explain how the TCP proxy, Linux pocket router, BBMD and BACnet router differ.
- Deliver one focused change or regression test with a reproducible before/after result and required repository checks.
- Identify resource ownership, shutdown behavior and data/management separation.
- Label offline, software topology, exact-image and physical MS/TP evidence independently.
- List open limitations and the next experiment without claiming certification or unsupported features.

## Deliverables

- PR-ready Rust/test change, concise problem/result description and verification evidence; publishing a PR is optional.
- A field runbook with topology, revisions, captures/logs, startup, shutdown and recovery.
- Portfolio links to the packet inspector, UDP messenger, TCP service/proxy, Modbus tool, BACnet explorer and Pi lab.
- A short oral/written walkthrough that another learner can follow without access to your memory.

Label every result **observed**, **fixture-only**, or **not run**. A failed case with a clear explanation is better evidence than an unsupported pass. Keep the baseline within the declared limits before attempting extensions.

## Self-review

- Which failure can you now localize to a layer instead of calling it “a BACnet timeout”?
- What remains uncertain, and what concrete measurement would settle it?
- Which small upstream contribution would you be comfortable explaining to its maintainer?

Explain your choices without reading your source aloud. If a criterion is missing, record a specific next experiment; do not silently redefine completion. Reference material is in [the reading list](SOURCES.md#week-16); references may contain examples, so attempt the review independently first.
