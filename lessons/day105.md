# Day 105 — Week 15 Review — serial field notebook

[Previous: Day 104](day104.md) · [Course index](INDEX.md) · [Lab guide](LAB_GUIDE.md) · [Next: Day 106](day106.md)

**Week 15 review · 2–4 hours, split across sessions as needed.** Prerequisites: Days 1–104, or equivalent skills. Reuse your earlier work rather than start every tool again.

## Project brief

Produce a Rust-backed serial diagnostic notebook explaining one healthy and one faulty scenario. Include offline RTU/MS/TP frame interpretation, lifecycle handling and token/timing reasoning. A hardware extension may use a separate bench, but a fixture-only completion must remain visibly fixture-only.

Environment: Dedicated Pi lab or the explicitly labeled VM/offline alternative. Hardware results must come from the actual hardware. Apply the [review rubric](LAB_GUIDE.md#review-rubric). This page intentionally contains no worked solution, implementation sequence, or companion implementation. Pick your own decomposition. You may consult language/API references and your earlier work.

## Acceptance criteria

- Distinguish UART, RS-485, RTU framing, MS/TP frames and BACnet application transactions.
- Demonstrate CRC/truncation/noise handling with independent known-answer fixtures.
- Prove bounded stop/reopen behavior in the tested environment.
- Explain a token cycle and at least one fault without overclaiming timestamp precision.
- Record adapter, framing, baud, station configuration, software version and exact scope of evidence.

## Deliverables

- Rust decoders/analyzers, tests and a labeled frame corpus or fixture references.
- Wiring/topology plan, timing notes, fault diagnosis and recovery/runbook.
- A gate table separating offline, PTY, real serial and physical multi-station results.

Label every result **observed**, **fixture-only**, or **not run**. A failed case with a clear explanation is better evidence than an unsupported pass. Keep the baseline within the declared limits before attempting extensions.

## Self-review

- Which claims are impossible to prove with a PCAP of only the Ethernet side?
- What is the smallest next experiment that would resolve the remaining uncertainty?

Explain your choices without reading your source aloud. If a criterion is missing, record a specific next experiment; do not silently redefine completion. Reference material is in [the reading list](SOURCES.md#week-15); references may contain examples, so attempt the review independently first.
