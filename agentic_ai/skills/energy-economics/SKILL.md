# Energy economics

Separate fuel energy, demand, fixed charges, incentives, carbon factors, and implementation cost. Identify whether each rate is verified, candidate, blended, or illustrative; do not use a blended rate without warning when demand charges matter.

State time period, escalation/sensitivity assumptions, emissions-factor source, and whether results are simple payback or lifecycle economics. Never invent installed cost, incentive, tariff assignment, or bill components absent from source evidence. Use `utility-tariff` for rate provenance.
